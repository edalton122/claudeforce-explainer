Step 4 · Enterprise Trust

Three pillars your InfoSec team will actually approve.

Claudeforce is designed for enterprise governance from the ground up — not bolted on after the fact.

Zero Data Retention

Every Claude inference call is stateless and isolated. No conversation history is stored. No customer data is logged. No records are used to train any AI model — by Anthropic or by Salesforce.

Enforced via Amazon Bedrock · Einstein Trust Layer

Enforced User Sharing Rules

Claude can only access records the requesting user is already authorized to see. Org-wide defaults, role hierarchies, and field-level security are all respected — automatically, at query time.

Built into the AIforce Interface Layer

Deterministic Action Guardrails

Every AI action is scoped, logged, and reversible. Admins define exactly what Claude can and cannot do — no open-ended agentic execution without explicit permission boundaries in place.
