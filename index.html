The Bottom-Line Efficiency Play

For CIOs tired of unmetered AI vendor lock-in, Claudeforce is a risk-reduction and cost-containment win — not just a features conversation.

Predictable Cost Model

Fixed 20 Flex Credits per action means Finance can budget AI spend like SaaS seats — not like a cloud compute bill. No surprise invoice at quarter end.

No New Attack Surface

Claude never touches your infrastructure. Zero Data Retention via Bedrock means no new data egress risk to model. Passes InfoSec review without a new vendor risk assessment.

Claudeforce is model-agnostic by design. Today it's Claude. Tomorrow it could be another frontier model. You own the orchestration layer — Salesforce does not lock you to one AI provider.
